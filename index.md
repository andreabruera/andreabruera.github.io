I am an **invited researcher (young PI for the EEG and language lab)** at the University of Coimbra, as part of the [Cogbooster](https://www.uc.pt/cogbooster/) team. 

My research deals mainly with words: deceivingly simple, yet extremely fascinating and powerful things! My main interest is investigating _how_ they mean _what_ they mean (good ol' **semantics**); however, since in language everything is connected, this inevitably leads me to deal with other topics within (syntax, discourse, phonology) and outside of language (statistical learning, vision, cognitive executive functions) as well.

I believe in a multidisciplinary approach, bringing together neuroscience, computer science, cognitive psychology and linguistics. Although I am specialized in (and most passionate about) electroencephalography (**EEG**) and cutting-edge methods from **machine learning**, Artificial Intelligence (**AI**), and in particular Natural Language Processing (**NLP**) - (large) language models, language statistics and distributions, corpora, I also use other techniques to study brain, cognition and language: mainly, functional magnetic resonance imaging (**fMRI**), transcranial magnetic stimulation (**TMS**) - and behavioural data.

Previously, I was a **postdoctoral researcher** at the [Max Planck Institute for Human Cognitive and Brain Sciences](https://www.cbs.mpg.de/en), in the [Cognition and Plasticity](./https://www.cbs.mpg.de/independent-research-groups/cognition-and-plasticity) research group led by [Gesa Hartwigsen](https://scholar.google.nl/citations?user=5YaFMKwAAAAJ&hl=de).

I received my **Ph.D. in Computer Science** from the School of Electronic Engineering and Computer Science at Queen Mary University of London, under the supervision of [Massimo Poesio](./https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=89aa1X0AAAAJ&hl=it&oi=ao) in the [CogSci group](./http://cogsci.eecs.qmul.ac.uk). 

If you'd like to collaborate, and/or come work with me in Coimbra, shoot me an email!
